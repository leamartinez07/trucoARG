**Description:**

Faltaenvidoytruco is a playable online version of Argentine Truco, built for friends to sit down and play immediately. Players can open a private room and share a link or code, join public rooms, or practice against bots. It supports 2, 4 and 6 players, 15- or 30-point matches, envido/real envido/falta envido, truco/retruco/vale cuatro, folds, pardas and automatic dealing between hands. The mobile interface includes a scorekeeper with matchstick tallies. Player profiles store match history and hand-by-hand replays. Chat is available in multiplayer rooms; voice/video uses Discord rather than an integrated call service.

The product is live at the demo URL. It uses React, TypeScript and Vite on the client, a Node.js/TypeScript game server, and PostgreSQL for persistent multiplayer rooms and history. The server shuffles with a cryptographic random generator, enforces turns and sends each player only their own hand. The codebase includes automated tests for game rules and online play.
